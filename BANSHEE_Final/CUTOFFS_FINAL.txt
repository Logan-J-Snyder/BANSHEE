WHAT WAS FINALIZED

No numerical cutoff changed. The 20s, 120s and 200s results are retained from
the November 9 calibration. Fresh fixed-size confirmation trials checked the
existing 600s and 1800s ln(4) cuts using BANSHEE_Final's frozen scientific code.
The 600s test now uses the same pre-injection transient-screening rule as the
other groups. All five groups pass their stated calibration checks.

The new 1800s result permits otherwise-passing events to be accepted. It does
not override morphology, edge-only follow-up, missing-stream validation or
parameter-quality decisions. Old output folders must not be resumed with this
changed version. Previously saved results are not automatically reclassified.

FIXED CONFIRMATION DESIGN

3,064 trials completed; zero failures. Per cadence (600s and 1800s):
- 1,152 controls: 192 each of quiet, impulse, step, reversed flare,
  symmetric bump and correlated noise.
- 300 natural-duration flare draws: 10 per sampled peak SNR 1 through 30.
- 80 separately reported long-flare challenges: 20 each at SNR 10, 15, 20 and 30.

Natural FWHM draws use the saved catalog-based 10-110 minute distribution;
long challenges use its >=45 minute subset. Three exposure-integrated Tovar
rise shapes are used. These are model-family injections, not a complete
empirical joint amplitude/duration/occurrence-rate model.

Both cutoffs and all jobs were frozen before outcomes. No cut was fitted to
these confirmation results. Every planned trial was retained; no additional
draws were added to obtain a pass. All placements were possible on their
initially assigned sources. Full 24-hour scored windows and substantial model
wings avoid the whole-curve and gap-segment 5% margins.

The run reused three 600s and four 1800s holdout background stars. Their random
injections/placements are fresh; the stars are not new or fully independent
of detector development. The previous failed 1800s check (12/384; 95% upper
bound 5.014%) is retained in the embedded history. It was not erased or pooled
with the new data to report the new bound.

INTERPRETATION

The 5% target describes at least one accepted event per screened 24-hour
control window under the equal-weight simulated control mixture. It is not
5% catalog contamination, a per-sector rate, or a separate bound for every
artifact kind. For example, 14/192 symmetric-bump controls passed at 1800s.
Overlapping windows and reused stars limit the interpretation of nominal
binomial intervals. These cuts are finalized for this release and tested
protocol, not guaranteed optimal for every stellar population or flux stream.

The plot and CUTOFFS_FINAL.json provide a compact portable record. The code
retains the full-precision cuts, prior 1800s result, and protocol/report hashes.
